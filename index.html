EMERGENCY GUIDE

What should you do during a landslide?

Simple instructions to help you stay safe before, during and after a landslide.

⚠️

Before a Landslide

  • Keep emergency supplies ready.
  • Know the safest evacuation route.
  • Monitor Ishana alerts and weather updates.
  • Avoid staying near unstable slopes.
🏃

During a Landslide

  • Move away from the landslide path immediately.
  • Do not try to cross moving debris.
  • Follow official evacuation instructions.
  • Help children and elderly people if it is safe.
🏠

After a Landslide

  • Stay away from the affected area.
  • Watch for additional landslides.
  • Do not return until authorities declare it safe.
  • Report blocked roads or dangerous conditions.
